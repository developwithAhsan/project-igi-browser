About

A simple fan-accessible place to explore the classic Project I.G.I. browser port.

Who we are

IGI Online is a fan-oriented website built around a browser-accessible Project I.G.I. experience. The site brings the game interface, practical setup guidance and background information together in one place.

Our mission

Our goal is to make it easier to understand how the browser port works, find the relevant controls and troubleshoot common startup or performance issues without changing the original game experience.

What the site offers

  • A browser-based WebAssembly game interface.
  • Keyboard, mouse and supported touch-control guidance.
  • Game facts, gameplay tips and frequently asked questions.
  • Contact details for technical feedback and rights-holder requests.

Project I.G.I. is a trademark of its respective rights holders. This site is an unofficial fan project.
