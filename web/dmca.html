Submit a takedown request

If you are a rights holder or authorized representative and believe that material hosted on this website infringes your rights, contact the site owner through the contact page. Include the exact URL, identification of the protected work, your relationship to the rights holder, a description of the allegedly infringing material, and sufficient information for us to respond.

Good-faith requests

Please provide accurate information and only submit requests you are authorized to make. We will review complete notices and take appropriate action where required by applicable law. A verified contact email will be published once supplied by the site owner.

Counter-notification

If content is removed following a notice and you believe the removal was mistaken, use the contact page to request review and provide relevant details. This page is general information, not legal advice.
