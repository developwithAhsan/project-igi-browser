How to play
✥Move — the stick on the left. Up/down also move through menus.
⌖Aim — swipe anywhere on the right. Hold FIRE and drag to aim while shooting.
◎Scope — SCOPE toggles the sniper scope and binocular zoom; ZOOM+ / ZOOM− fine-tune it.
⇅Walk / crouch — WALK and CROUCH toggle (lit = on). Project I.G.I. has no prone.
⚙Settings — swipe sensitivity, scope sensitivity, gyroscope aiming (turn the phone to aim).
☰Menu — MENU opens the game menu. Leave its Configuration keys at default, the buttons depend on them.
Press Alt + Enter for fullscreen
