02 / ABOUT THE GAME

Enter the world of Project I.G.I.

Project I.G.I.: I'm Going In is a first-person tactical stealth shooter originally released in 2000. You play as David Jones, a former SAS soldier working through high-risk missions that reward observation, planning and controlled action.

Use the environment, long-range weapons, equipment and careful movement to infiltrate enemy positions. Its large outdoor areas and mission-based structure helped make it a memorable PC shooter.

How to play
✥Move — the stick on the left. Push forward and slide your thumb up onto the runner to lock running; tap the stick to stop. WALK: tap to walk, tap again to run.
⌖Aim — swipe anywhere in the middle. Hold either FIRE and drag to aim while shooting.
◎Scope — tap the eye to snap the sniper scope or binoculars to 4× and back; + / − fine-tune.
⇅Crouch / jump / peek — around FIRE. Tap CROUCH to crouch, tap again to stand. There is no prone in this game.
⚙Settings — sensitivity, scope zoom, gyroscope, and Edit layout to drag the buttons where you like. MENU pauses; tap the menu items as usual.
⟲Gyroscope — turn the phone to fine-aim while scoped, like PUBG. Your browser may ask for motion access.

    05 / TIPS & TRICKS

    Practical Project I.G.I. mission tips

    • Use binoculars to scout guards and the route ahead before crossing an exposed area.
    • Watch for security cameras and use available computer terminals to interfere with surveillance systems.
    • Choose cover and a quiet approach where possible; open firefights make stealth harder.
    • Plan each objective before engaging. The original campaign contains 14 missions and does not allow saving midway through a mission.
    • Health recovery depends on finding a medical kit; do not assume that taking cover alone restores health.
    • Keep a backup of your own game folder and avoid clearing the browser storage that holds the imported copy.
